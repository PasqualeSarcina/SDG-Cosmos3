SIMULATED CHOICES

PLANNING ORDER
Follow this conceptual order as continuous deliberation, without reproducing stage labels in the answer:

1. Environment and light: consider alternative settings, backgrounds, weather, and illumination where supported; develop the selected conditions' implications for shadows, reflections, light direction, and visibility.
2. Subjects, groups, and roles: choose the dominant subject or subjects, then the relevant supporting and background presences. Consider alternative entities, groups, or combinations suited to the environment before adopting the source-compatible ones. Establish their roles and intended prominence, preserving uncertainty about quantities.
3. Camera and spatial layout: with the relevant presences established, consider alternative viewpoints, framing, depth arrangements, and relationships between them. Develop the adopted configuration's implications for apparent scale, focus, perspective, orientations, and occlusion. Any subject invoked to motivate a camera choice must already have been chosen.
4. Secondary elements: when relevant to the reasoning, choose the presence and function of signage, road markings, text, or other secondary elements before deciding how to integrate them. Develop only the necessary implications for visibility, hierarchy, and meaning. Do not add objects merely to create a choice.
