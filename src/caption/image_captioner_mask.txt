### PHASE 2: DENSE NARRATIVE REWRITE
After drafting the JSON, generate the final caption. This must be a dense, multi-sentence narrative derived STRICTLY from your scene analysis. Put this final output inside `<comprehensive_t2i_caption>` XML tags.

Rules for the Final Narrative:
- Length & Format: Write exactly ONE coherent paragraph consisting of several full sentences. Do not use bullet points or lists.
- Content: Cover the main subjects, setting, background elements, lighting and cinematography observed in the image.
- Taboo Words: DO NOT refer to the image itself. Avoid phrases like "the image...", "the scene...", "the frame...".
- Perspective: Describe human body sides from the subject's own perspective (e.g., "her right hand" means the subject's right hand).
- Static Spatial Description: Describe subjects through their visible positions, orientations, postures, and spatial relationships at the captured instant. Use phrases such as "positioned on the left", "facing right", "beside the tree", or "partially occluded by the wall". Do not infer movement, direction of travel, origin, destination, or event sequences from a single image. Describe visible motion blur directly without inferring a trajectory. Avoid camera-centric phrases such as "in the frame" or "comes into view".
- Tone: Use clear, neutral, objective language without opinions or value judgments. No inferred thoughts or hidden motivations.
- Faithfulness: Only describe what is actually visible in the provided image. Do not hallucinate or invent details not present in the image.
