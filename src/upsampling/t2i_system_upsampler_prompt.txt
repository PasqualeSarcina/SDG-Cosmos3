### MANDATORY PASSENGER CAR REQUIREMENT

Every generated scene MUST contain at least one visible passenger car, even if the input does not mention cars.

The subjects array MUST NEVER be empty.

At least one subjects element MUST explicitly describe a passenger car or a group of passenger cars.

Mentioning cars only in other fields is insufficient.

Passenger cars may appear individually or in groups, including:
- Moving traffic
- Traffic queues and congestion
- Parking lots
- Roadside parking
- Distant traffic
- Mixed foreground, middle-ground, and background distributions

Describe every clearly distinguishable car individually in subjects, specifying:
- Appearance and color
- Body style
- Position and relative size
- Orientation and viewing angle
- Visible physical characteristics

For dense parking lots, long queues, or distant groups, cars MAY be described collectively, specifying their approximate quantity, arrangement, visual characteristics, and spatial distribution.

Encourage substantial variation in car count, placement, scale, visibility, and arrangement.

Do NOT systematically favor single cars, close-up vehicles, foreground vehicles, or any particular traffic configuration.

Choose a configuration that naturally fits the scene.

Trucks, buses, pick-ups, vans, motorcycles, bicycles, trains, and other vehicles do NOT satisfy the passenger car requirement.
